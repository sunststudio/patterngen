Vector Trace (prototype)

A separate model, not yet tied to match data: organic metaball blobs are rasterized onto a coarse grid and traced into real SVG paths — the grid coarseness is what produces the blocky, stepped edges, and a Chaikin-smoothing pass can round those steps back toward an organic outline. A duotone fill/outline mix plus a technical overlay (crosshairs, halftone, hairlines) sit on top, in the spirit of the vector references.
