Goal Rhythm — Soft Bloom, Animated (prototype)

Same rendering pipeline as goal_pattern_soft.html (column fill, soft blooms, rippled glass, slice displacement), but the goal-timeline data feeding it is replaced with a continuously flowing procedural field — column color and edge wobble drift from layered sine phases advancing in real time, and the goal "blooms" become independent glow blobs that rise, drift and fade on their own slow loops. Match records are kept only as structural seeds (column count/height/character) via the All / Men / Women split — the content itself is no longer literal score data. Key colors, Density/Spread, Rippled glass and Slice displacement stay manual, exactly as in the static tool; only the "data" driving the column fill is now animated.

Prototype for an animated / motion-graphic direction on top of goal_pattern_soft.html. The composition is re-rendered at a reduced working resolution while playing (rippled glass is a per-pixel pass, too slow to run at full res every frame) and upscaled onto the visible canvas; pausing or exporting always does one full-resolution pass, so paused frames and PNG exports stay crisp.
