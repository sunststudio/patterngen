Line Field Generator (prototype)

Generates a field of straight or curved lines, an abstract take on tactics-board diagrams. Points can sit on an even grid, scatter randomly, or converge into one or more organic bundles — slide the Clustering control to mix scatter and bundles freely, and pick a bundle shape (blob, line, L, U) so clusters read as more than a single radial burst. Overlapping strokes multiply-blend so dense areas read darker. Arrowheads and dots are optional.

Prototype for the abstract line-field direction off lines-1/2/3.png. Length clumping biases each scatter line's length toward a few randomly placed short/long "zones" instead of sampling Min/Max independently per line — 0% is a flat random mix everywhere, 100% produces visible patches of short lines in one area and long ones in another. In Organic mode, a line is either drawn from the bundle structure (probability = Clustering%) or as an independent random scatter line — Min/Max length and clumping only drive the scatter half, since a bundled line's length and direction fall out of its origin zone's distance from its bundle's pocket instead. Density comes from thickness + opacity + multiply blend rather than a separate accumulation pass.
