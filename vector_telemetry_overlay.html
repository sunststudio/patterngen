Vector Telemetry Overlay (prototype)

Reads vector-2.png as three layers rather than the whole picture: vertical hairline hatch bands, crosshair/tag marks, and monospace "smallprint" readouts. The large black silhouette shapes in the reference are deliberately left out for now — this prototype is only about the line/mark/text texture on top. Readout content is pulled from vector-filler-text.md (GPS/EPTS, xG/possession, VAR calibration, ball-chip telemetry); short tag codes next to crosshairs are procedurally generated in the same style.
